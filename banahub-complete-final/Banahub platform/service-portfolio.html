Service — Investors

Portfolio Growth Advisory

The client problem

Investors often have portfolio companies with genuine market opportunity but without the commercial or regional expertise to execute. Providing advisory capital alongside financial capital improves portfolio company outcomes without requiring investors to build in-house operating teams.

Who this is for

Venture capital firms, family offices, and private equity investors with portfolio companies entering or expanding in Southeast Asia, the Gulf, or North Asia. Engaged on behalf of the investor or directly with the portfolio company with investor consent.

Scope

Same structured advisory services as company-direct engagements — GTM strategy, market entry, commercial pipeline development — commissioned by or in coordination with the portfolio company's investors.

Deliverables

Defined per engagement based on portfolio company's specific needs. All deliverables are written and shared with the commissioning investor.

Timeline

Structured per portfolio company engagement. Typically 90 days to 6 months.

Engagement model

Monthly advisory fee per portfolio company, or programme fee covering multiple companies. All terms confirmed in writing.

What is not included

This service does not include making investment decisions, providing investment advice, or conducting regulated activities on behalf of the investor or portfolio company.

warning Advisory work does not guarantee commercial outcomes for portfolio companies. Results depend on execution by the portfolio company and market conditions.
