What membership is — and is not

Growth and Scale memberships provide community, briefings, programme access and advisory time. They do not include, and do not buy, investor introductions, investment opportunities, fundraising outcomes or financial advice. Investor Circle participation is by application, approved manually, and does not oblige any party to invest or meet. BANAHUB is not licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. See Disclosures and Terms.

Questions

Can I pay by PayNow every month?

Yes. Choose PayNow at checkout. Stripe emails you an invoice at the start of each period with a PayNow QR code (card also accepted on the invoice). You have 7 days to pay each renewal invoice before access pauses.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing and choose Manage billing. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. We do not refund partial periods unless required by law.

Can I switch between monthly and annual, or between Growth and Scale?

Yes — contact hello@banahub.com and we will move your subscription. Card members can also manage their plan from Billing.

Do you issue invoices for company expense claims?

Every payment has a Stripe invoice and receipt in your organisation's name, downloadable from Billing.

Is the Investor Circle paid?

It is complimentary while in pilot and open by application only to professional investors (VC, PE, family offices, corporate venture, institutional and selected angels). Each application is reviewed by the BANAHUB team.
