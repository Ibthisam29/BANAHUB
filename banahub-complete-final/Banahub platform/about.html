What we are

An advisory firm — not a fund, broker or licensed financial adviser

BANAHUB is a Singapore-registered advisory firm. We work with a limited number of clients at any one time so that each engagement receives senior attention.

We are not a venture fund and do not invest. We are not licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore and do not provide regulated financial advice, deal in securities or arrange offers of securities. Where we introduce companies to investors, we do so as an advisory firm, only to investors who meet applicable eligibility requirements, and on terms set out in a written engagement agreement.

Every engagement has defined deliverables, a timeline and agreed fees. We do not run open-ended retainers without outputs, and we do not commit to outcomes we cannot control.

How we work

What we do, and what we do not do

We do

  • Scope engagements with defined outputs before work begins
  • Give an honest assessment of readiness and fit
  • Prepare companies for investor conversations
  • Introduce counterparties only where mandates and eligibility align
  • Coordinate with licensed legal, tax and corporate providers where needed

We do not

  • Guarantee capital, investor meetings or commercial outcomes
  • Act as a licensed financial adviser, broker or placement agent
  • Publish unverified metrics about our network or track record
  • Share client information without consent
  • Take equity in place of fees without a separate written agreement
