Regulatory status

Bana Private Limited (trading as BANAHUB) is not licensed, authorised, or regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) or any other financial services regulator to provide regulated financial advice, conduct capital markets activities, or act as a placement agent, broker, dealer, or fund manager.

All services provided by BANAHUB are advisory services only, provided under a commercial advisory agreement. Nothing BANAHUB does constitutes the provision of regulated financial advice, the solicitation of capital market investments, or the management of any investment portfolio.

Introduction services

Where BANAHUB facilitates introductions between companies and investors, it does so as a non-regulated advisory intermediary only. BANAHUB does not receive commission, success fees, or brokerage in connection with the completion of any investment transaction unless expressly agreed in a separate written agreement. BANAHUB does not guarantee that any introduction will result in a meeting, a term sheet, or an investment.

No guarantee of results

BANAHUB's advisory services — including GTM engagements, investor readiness, and market entry support — are professional advisory services. They do not and cannot guarantee commercial outcomes, revenue, investment, or market access. Past engagements do not predict future results for any client.

Capital Access services

Capital Access work — capital readiness, investment narrative, fundraising strategy, investor targeting and investor campaign support — is advisory work carried out for the company under a written engagement agreement. It does not constitute a public offer of securities, a placement, underwriting or arranging of any offer. Where BANAHUB introduces a company to an investor, the introduction is made only to investors who meet applicable eligibility requirements, only with the company's consent, and does not guarantee a meeting, term sheet or investment. Any success fee is payable only where expressly agreed in that written agreement and permitted by law.

Investor network

Joining the BANAHUB investor network is free and creates no obligation. BANAHUB may share short information briefs about companies whose profile fits an investor's stated preferences. BANAHUB does not assess the merits of any investment for an investor, make recommendations, or act on an investor's behalf. Investors must make their own assessment and take their own professional advice.

Membership

The BANAHUB membership ($8/month or $88/year) provides member-portal access, priority event registration, programme applications and member resources. It does not include investor introductions, access to investment opportunities, regulated services or advisory work. Advisory engagements are separate and subject to their own eligibility criteria and agreements.
