Service — Advisory Intermediary

Investor Introductions

The client problem

Companies that have completed investor readiness work and meet specific eligibility criteria may seek warm introductions to investors whose mandate aligns with their raise. Generic outreach to investors without mandate alignment is inefficient and reputationally damaging.

Who this is for

Companies must have: (1) completed investor readiness work — internally or with BANAHub; (2) a clear and documented raise amount, structure, and use of funds; (3) operating history and demonstrable traction; (4) a mandate match with investors in the BANAHub network. Eligibility is assessed case by case. Not all companies that enquire will be eligible.

Scope

Where eligibility criteria are met, BANAHub facilitates introductions between companies and investors whose stated mandate aligns with the raise. We do not conduct mass outreach. Introductions are made individually and are accompanied by a brief prepared by BANAHub.

Deliverables

Mandate assessment against network. Preparation of introduction brief. Facilitation of warm introduction. Initial follow-up support.

Timeline

Assessed within 2–4 weeks of completed eligibility review.

Engagement model

Advisory fee structure. BANAHub does not operate as a placement agent or broker. Any introduction fee arrangement must be agreed in writing and is separate from advisory fees.

What is not included

Introductions are not guaranteed. BANAHub cannot guarantee that any introduction will result in a meeting, a term sheet, or investment. BANAHub is not a licensed placement agent, broker, or financial adviser. This service does not constitute regulated financial advice or securities dealing.

warning BANAHub is not licensed by MAS or any other regulator to conduct regulated capital market activity. Introductions are facilitated on a non-regulated advisory basis only. Nothing in this service constitutes a solicitation to invest.
