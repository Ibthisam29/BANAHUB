Questions

For investors

Is there a fee to join the investor network?

No. There is no fee and no obligation to review or invest in any opportunity.

Who is eligible?

Professional and institutional investors, family offices, corporate investors and individual investors who meet applicable eligibility requirements — for example, accredited investor status under Singapore's Securities and Futures Act. We may ask you to confirm your status before sharing opportunities.

Do you recommend investments?

No. We share information about companies whose profile fits your stated mandate. We do not give advice on the merits of any investment, and you should make your own assessment and take your own advice.

How is my information used?

Your profile is used only to match opportunities to your mandate and is never shared with companies without your consent. See our Privacy Policy.

Do you work with family offices and multi-family offices?

Yes. Single-family and multi-family offices allocating to private markets can share each family's mandate — sectors, stages, regions and ticket size — and receive only direct opportunities that fit it, with full materials shared only with the company's consent.

Do you work with private equity firms and hedge funds?

Yes. For private equity, growth, crossover and hedge fund investors, BANAHUB shares growth-stage and pre-IPO opportunities that fit the stated mandate, gives commercial and market-entry input on targets in Singapore and ASEAN, and supports portfolio companies with go-to-market and partnership work.

Can LPs, fund of funds and asset managers join the investor network?

Yes. LPs and institutional investors (such as pensions, endowments, sovereign funds and insurers), fund of funds and asset managers can join for free and share their mandate. The same eligibility checks apply, and BANAHUB does not provide investment advice or recommendations.

What kind of opportunities do you share?

Companies that have completed a BANAHUB capital review and fit your stated sector, stage, geography and ticket size. You first receive a short anonymised brief; the company's full materials are shared only with its consent, and diligence, terms and decisions stay between you and the company.
