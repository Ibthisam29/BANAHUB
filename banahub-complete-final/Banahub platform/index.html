Engagement principles

What you can expect from us

Trust in an advisory firm comes from clear scope and plain statements about what we can and cannot do.

  • Defined scope, deliverables and fees before work starts
  • An honest view of readiness and fit — including when the answer is "not yet"
  • Introductions only where a counterparty's stated mandate and eligibility align
  • Confidentiality by default, with NDAs available on request
  • No guarantees of investment, meetings or commercial results
  • No regulated financial advice or securities dealing
  • No unverified claims about networks or track record
  • No open-ended retainers without outputs
