Service — Companies

Investor Readiness

The client problem

Many companies approaching investors are not commercially or narratively ready — their financial model is unclear, their investment narrative is weak, or their due diligence materials are incomplete. This costs time and credibility with the investors that matter.

Who this is for

Companies that have made a decision to raise capital and need to prepare their materials and narrative to a standard that institutional investors expect. Typically Series A and above, or companies raising a significant seed round from professional investors.

Scope

Review and strengthening of investment narrative, financial model, pitch deck, and supporting due diligence materials. Advisory on positioning, valuation framing, and investor Q&A preparation. Does not include making introductions to investors — that is a separate engagement with separate eligibility criteria.

Deliverables

Written investment narrative. Reviewed and annotated pitch deck with rewrite recommendations. Financial model review notes. Due diligence checklist and gap assessment. Q&A preparation notes.

Timeline

4–8 weeks depending on current state of materials and responsiveness.

Engagement model

Fixed fee, typically structured in two phases: diagnostic and materials review, then narrative development and revision.

What is not included

This service does not include investor introductions. Completing this service does not guarantee access to BANAHub's network or any introduction to any investor.

warning BANAHub does not guarantee that investor-ready materials will result in investment or investor meetings. Capital raising outcomes depend on market conditions, investor appetite, and many factors outside any adviser's control.
