Process

How a market-entry engagement runs

Diagnose

Current position, product-market fit evidence, resources and the outcome leadership needs from the new market.

Decide

Market priority, entry model, target segments and the partners or channels to pursue first.

Build

Positioning, sales materials, partner outreach and pipeline development, with weekly progress tracking.

Scale

Review of early results, adjustment of the plan and preparation for the next market or capital conversation.

Questions

Before you get in touch

Do you help with company incorporation and licensing?

We are not a corporate secretarial, legal or tax firm. We help you decide on the right set-up and coordinate with licensed local providers, who carry out the regulated work.

How long does a market-entry engagement take?

It depends on the number of markets and the depth of support. The diagnostic and entry plan is scoped with a fixed timeline before work starts; execution support — partner outreach, pipeline building, channel development — is scoped in phases with a review at the end of each.

Can you introduce us to customers or partners?

Where there is a clear fit, yes. Introductions are made on the basis of a defined target list agreed with you, and we do not promise specific meetings or contracts.

How are fees structured?

Fixed fees for defined diagnostic and strategy work, and phased fees for execution support. Any performance-linked element is agreed in writing at the start of the engagement.

Planning your entry into Singapore or ASEAN?

Tell us about the business and the markets you are considering. We reply within two business days.
