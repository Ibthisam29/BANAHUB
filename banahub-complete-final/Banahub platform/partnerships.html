Process

From proposal to delivery

Proposal

You share the objective, audience and timing. We reply with an initial view within two business days.

Design

Joint scoping of format, roles, budget and how success will be measured.

Agreement

A written agreement covering deliverables, commercial terms, branding and data handling.

Delivery and reporting

Execution against the plan, with a written report on outcomes at the end.

Questions

Partnership questions

What kinds of organisations partner with BANAHUB?

Corporates, government and investment agencies, trade and industry bodies, accelerators, universities, professional service firms and event organisers that want to move companies across borders into or out of Singapore and ASEAN.

What does a BANAHUB market-access programme include?

A structured programme for a cohort of companies entering Singapore or ASEAN: selection, diagnostics, workshops, partner meetings and progress reporting to the sponsoring organisation, under a written agreement covering deliverables, commercial terms, branding and data handling.

How quickly does BANAHUB respond to partnership proposals?

Within two business days, with an initial view on fit, format and next steps.
