GTM & Market Entry

The client problem

Growth-stage companies often have a product and some early commercial traction but lack the structured commercial strategy, channel expertise, or regional relationships to enter a new market effectively. Market entry without structure wastes time and capital.

Who this is for

Companies with a working product or service, at least some existing revenue or validated demand, and a clear hypothesis about which market to enter. We do not work with pre-revenue startups or companies at idea stage.

Scope

Structured advisory covering market assessment, go-to-market strategy definition, channel identification, partner outreach, and commercial pipeline development in a target market across SEA, the Gulf, or North Asia.

Deliverables

Written market assessment. GTM strategy document with prioritised channels and actions. Partner and channel longlist with outreach approach. Fortnightly advisory sessions with written notes. End-of-engagement review and next-step recommendations.

Timeline

Growth Diagnostic: 2–3 weeks. 90-Day GTM Sprint: 90 days. Cross-Border Expansion: 3–6 months with defined milestones.

Engagement model

Fixed fee for Diagnostic. Monthly advisory fee or fixed project fee for Sprint and Expansion. Scope and fee confirmed in writing before work begins.

What is not included

Investor introductions are not included and are not guaranteed as part of GTM engagements. Introductions are a separate service where eligibility criteria apply.

warning BANAHub does not guarantee commercial outcomes, partnerships, or revenue results. Advisory work improves your strategic position — outcomes depend on your execution.
